Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 (Google Calendar added)

Shaft is a personal tool used only by its owner, Daniele Grassi, with his own Google accounts. No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, with the read-only scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: only the threads under labels he chooses, and only the calendars he chooses. It never sends, deletes, labels or marks mail as read, and never creates, changes or answers calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Mail and calendar content is read on demand by the owner's own software assistants running on that computer, and may be processed by the AI model providers those assistants use, solely to do work the owner asked for.
  • Attachments the owner's assistants download stay on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties for any other purpose, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Shaft's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
