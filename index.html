Shaft

Shaft is a personal tool run by Daniele Grassi on his own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

With his permission, it reads the Gmail threads that carry labels he chooses, and the Google Calendar calendars he chooses, in his own Google accounts, so his own software assistants can work on them. Access is read-only (gmail.readonly, calendar.readonly): it never sends, deletes, labels or marks mail, and never creates, changes or answers events.
